# UndertaleInProccessing
A rip of undertale, remade in processing. Yay!


## CONTROLS
- Arrow Keys / WASD to move around. Enter the door on the right to proceed to the next room.

## DIRECTIONS
- After going in the second room, go up to the grass patch to initiate the dialogue cutscene.

This is more of a "proof of concept of undertale functions in processing" than an actual game. It was difficult to do, but it is a strong foundation for future endevours, like making a whole boss inside of processing.
